## Hello and welcome to my page

Binh Hoang Tieu (PhD) received the bachelor's degree in Information Technology from Hanoi National University, Vietnam, and the Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from the College of Engineering and Technology, Vietnam National University, Hanoi. He is currently a lecturer and researcher in the field of computer science and information technology. His research focuses on artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, educational data mining, learning analytics, natural language processing, and intelligent tutoring systems. <br><br>
